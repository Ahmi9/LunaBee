About

Hi, I'm Alexia.

My name is Alexia — friends call me Lexi. I love all things nature, folk songs, and finding the good in everything 🤍

The name "Luna Bee" comes from my dad and my dog. My dad passed unexpectedly in 2017, which flipped my world upside down. I found my dog in 2019, and she has flipped my world upside down in the most beautiful way. Everything I build here is an ode to them.

Photography has become my way of holding onto the good — the quiet in-between moments, the warm light, the people I love. I'm so grateful you're here, and I can't wait to watch this little dream grow 🤍

— Alexia (Lexi) 🤍
